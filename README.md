## ✨ About the Project

**SGIT AUTONOMOUS — Student Career & Learning Hub** is a full-stack digital platform I designed and developed for my college, **Dr. Samuel George Institute of Engineering & Technology (SGIT), Markapur**.

Instead of being just a traditional college website, this platform brings the college's public information and student-focused digital services together into one modern ecosystem.

The goal is simple:

> **Give SGIT students one place to learn, track academics, prepare for careers, discover opportunities and build their professional profile.**

This project was built with a strong focus on **premium UI/UX, role-based access, academic persistence, career preparation and scalable architecture**.

## 🎓 Student Portal

After student authentication, the experience changes into a dedicated student portal with a professional sidebar-based dashboard.

Students can access:

- 📊 Academic Progress
- 🧮 SGIT CGPA Calculator
- 📄 ATS-Friendly Resume Maker
- 📑 CV Maker
- ✉️ Cover Letter Maker
- 🤖 ATS Score Checker
- 🎤 Mock Interview
- 💼 Internship Discovery
- 💡 Major & Minor Project Ideas
- 💻 Technical Skills & Roadmaps
- ▶️ YouTube Learning Resources
- 🗓️ Workshops
- 🏅 Certificates
- 🔖 Bookmarks
- 🔔 Notifications
- 👤 Student Profile
